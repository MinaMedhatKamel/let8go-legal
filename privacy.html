Let8Go — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Let8Go (“we”, “us”) operates the Let8Go mobile game (the “App”). This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Diagnostics & crash data — via Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) to keep the App stable and understand feature usage. This is pseudonymous.
  • Device identifiers — used for analytics and, in competitive mode, matchmaking.
  • Push notification token — via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and APNs (iOS) to send match and turn notifications, if you enable notifications.
  • Competitive mode (Skillz) — the App offers both free-play and optional real-money (cash entry) competitions powered by the Skillz SDK (a third party). If you play a Skillz match, Skillz may collect device information, identifiers, and — for cash competitions — location and payment-related data to verify eligibility, run competitions, and process entries and prizes. Skillz processes this under its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not sell your personal data.

How we use it

To operate and improve the game, fix crashes, deliver notifications you opt into, and run free and paid competitive matches.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — privacy info
  • Skillz (competitive mode) — privacy, terms

Children

The App is rated for adults, is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Real-money competitions are restricted to eligible adults in permitted regions.

Your choices & rights

You can disable push notifications in your device settings. To request access to or deletion of data we hold, email us at let8go2026@gmail.com. Skillz-held data is handled through Skillz’s own request process.

Data retention & security

Data is encrypted in transit. We retain diagnostic data only as long as needed for the purposes above.
