Let8Go — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Let8Go (“we”, “us”) operates the Let8Go mobile game (the “App”) on Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and readable.

Who controls your information

The data controller for information collected through the App is:

Mina Medhat Kamel, trading as Let8Go
Berlin, Germany
Email: let8go2026@gmail.com

For any privacy question or request, email us at the address above. We do not operate a separate postal privacy desk — email is the fastest way to reach us.

Where a third-party provider decides for itself how information is processed — most notably Skillz for competitive tournaments — that provider acts as its own controller for that information, under its own privacy policy.

Where Let8Go is available

The Let8Go puzzle game is offered worldwide, subject to the app stores’ own country availability.

Competitive play is different. Skillz tournaments — and in particular real-money (cash entry) competitions — are only available in the countries, states and territories where Skillz supports them and where local law permits skill-based competition for prizes. Where cash play is not permitted, the App is playable in free-play mode only, or competitive features may not appear at all.

This is why location and eligibility information is processed in competitive mode: Skillz uses it to determine whether you are in a permitted region and eligible to enter. The current list of permitted regions is maintained by Skillz, not by us — see the Skillz terms for the applicable eligibility rules.

Information we collect

  • Diagnostics & crash data — via Google Firebase, to keep the App stable and understand feature usage. This is pseudonymous. See the two Firebase sections below.
  • Device identifiers — used for analytics and, in competitive mode, matchmaking.
  • Push notification token — via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and APNs (iOS), so we can send you notifications if you enable them.
  • Competitive mode (Skillz) — the App offers both free-play and optional real-money (cash entry) competitions powered by the Skillz SDK (a third party). If you play a Skillz match, the Skillz SDK may collect device information, identifiers, and — for cash competitions — location and payment-related data to verify eligibility, run competitions, and process entries and prizes. Skillz processes this under its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not ask you to create a Let8Go account, and we do not collect your name, postal address or payment details directly. Any account you create for competitive play is a Skillz account, held by Skillz.

Firebase Analytics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used, so we can improve it. Analytics may collect:

  • Gameplay events — for example starting a game, completing a level, or reaching a result screen.
  • Session information — when the App is opened, how long a session lasts, and how often the App is used.
  • App interactions — which screens and features are used, and general navigation patterns.
  • Device identifiers — pseudonymous app-instance identifiers used to group events from the same installation.
  • Technical information — device model, operating system version, language, region and general app configuration.

This information is used to measure feature usage, find where players get stuck, and prioritise improvements. We use it in aggregate; it is not used to identify you personally.

Firebase Crashlytics

The App uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes and errors. This is separate from analytics and is used only for stability and diagnostics. Crash reports may include:

  • Crash logs and stack traces describing what the App was doing when it failed.
  • Operating system version and device model.
  • Diagnostic information such as available memory, app version, and the sequence of non-personal events leading up to the crash.

We use crash reports solely to reproduce and fix problems in the App.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and APNs (iOS) to send you:

  • Tournament updates — results, status changes, and outcomes of competitions you entered.
  • Competition reminders — for example when an event you can enter is starting or ending.
  • Gameplay notifications — such as your turn in a match, or new content in the game.
  • Important account or service information — for example changes that affect your ability to play.

Notifications are optional. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings (Android: App info → Notifications; iOS: Settings → Notifications → Let8Go). Turning them off does not stop you from playing.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate the game and its core features;
  • fix crashes, errors and performance problems;
  • understand feature usage and improve the App;
  • deliver notifications you have opted into;
  • run competitive matches and tournaments;
  • protect against fraud, cheating and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for push notifications and, where required in your region, for analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App and, for competitive play, to run tournaments you choose to enter.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the App secure and stable, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the game.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We share information only in these situations:

  • With service providers that operate the game. These providers process information on our behalf, or as independent controllers of their own platforms, so the App can function. They include:
    • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) — privacy info
    • Skillz (competitive mode only) — privacy policy
    • Google Play Services (Android platform services) — privacy policy
  • Each provider processes information under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to read the policies linked above; where a provider acts as its own controller, its policy governs that processing.
  • Where legally required. We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a public authority, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect the rights and safety of players and the public.

If Let8Go is ever involved in a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, and we will state so in this policy.

International transfers

Some of our service providers process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from those where you live.

Where transfers of personal data out of the EU/EEA or UK take place, we and our providers rely on the safeguards required by law — such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or equivalent mechanisms operated by the provider. Google and Skillz publish their own transfer safeguards in the policies linked above.

Data retention

How long information is kept depends on:

  • the purpose it was collected for;
  • legal obligations that require us to keep certain records;
  • fraud prevention and dispute resolution needs;
  • security and abuse investigation needs;
  • provider configuration — how long the platform storing the data is set to retain it.

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as long as the law requires. We do not state fixed retention periods here because they differ by data type and provider.

  • Firebase retention follows the retention settings configured for our Firebase project. Analytics and crash data are pseudonymous and are retained according to that configuration and Google’s own policies.
  • Skillz manages the retention of tournament, account and transaction information according to its own policies and its legal and financial obligations. We do not control those retention periods.

Account & data deletion

You can ask us to delete the information Let8Go controls at any time by emailing let8go2026@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will confirm receipt and respond within the time limits set by applicable law (under the GDPR, normally within one month).

Because the App does not require a Let8Go account, we may ask you for information that helps us locate the relevant records — for example your app instance identifier or device details. If we cannot identify any data relating to you, we will tell you.

If you play Skillz tournaments, your competitive account is held by Skillz, not by us. To delete a Skillz account and the information associated with it, use Skillz’s own account management or support process (skillz.com/privacy). We can pass a request on, but we cannot delete a Skillz account on your behalf.

Some information may be retained after a deletion request where retention is required for:

  • legal obligations;
  • fraud prevention and platform integrity;
  • financial regulations, including records relating to entry fees and prizes.

Where we retain information for these reasons, we keep only what is necessary and stop using it for any other purpose.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — and in full if you are in the EU/EEA or UK — you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access — ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your personal data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to the exceptions described above.
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to pause the use of your data while a question about it is being resolved.
  • Right to object — object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests, including profiling.
  • Right to data portability — receive the data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or ask us to transfer it to another provider where technically feasible.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (for example push notifications or analytics), withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing that already took place.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — complain to a data protection supervisory authority. If you are in Germany, this is the authority for your federal state; if you are elsewhere in the EU/EEA or UK, it is the authority where you live, work, or where the issue occurred.

To exercise any of these rights, email let8go2026@gmail.com. Exercising them is free, and we will not treat you differently for doing so. Rights over data held by Skillz should be directed to Skillz through its own request process.

Regional privacy information

Because Let8Go is available in many countries, some regions give you additional or differently named rights. The rights listed above apply to everyone we can practically extend them to. The notes below cover the specifics.

European Economic Area

The rights listed above apply in full. You may complain to the supervisory authority in your EU member state. As our establishment is in Berlin, Germany, the competent German authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

United Kingdom

The same rights apply under the UK GDPR. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Switzerland

Swiss residents have equivalent rights under the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

California

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. California residents may request to know, delete, or correct personal information, and may not be discriminated against for doing so. The categories we handle are identifiers (pseudonymous device and app-instance IDs), internet or network activity (app usage), and — in competitive mode, via Skillz — geolocation and commercial information. You may use an authorised agent to submit a request.

Other United States state privacy laws

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others as they take effect) have rights to access, correct, delete and port their personal data, to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling, and to appeal a refused request. We do not conduct targeted advertising, sale, or profiling with legal effects. Email us to exercise these rights or to appeal.

Brazil

Under the LGPD, you have rights of confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent. The supervisory authority is the ANPD.

Canada

Under PIPEDA and equivalent provincial laws, you may access and correct your personal information and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Australia

Under the Privacy Act and the Australian Privacy Principles, you may access and correct your personal information and complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Japan and South Korea

Japanese users have rights of disclosure, correction and suspension of use under the APPI. Korean users have rights of access, correction, deletion and suspension of processing, and to withdraw consent, under PIPA.

India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may access, correct and erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance with us before approaching the Data Protection Board.

Elsewhere

If your country’s law gives you privacy rights not listed here, email us — we will honour any request we are legally required to honour, and we will try to honour reasonable requests even where we are not.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

The minimum age at which a person can consent to their data being processed varies by country — in the EU/EEA it is set by each member state at between 13 and 16, and other countries set their own thresholds. Where your country sets a higher age than 13, that higher age applies to you, and processing that relies on consent requires the consent of a parent or guardian below it.

Real-money competitions are additionally restricted to adults who meet Skillz’s own eligibility rules in permitted regions. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email let8go2026@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information handled by the App, including:

  • Encryption in transit — data sent between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit where the platform supports it (the Firebase and Skillz SDKs use encrypted connections by default).
  • Limited access — we keep the amount of information we handle to a minimum and restrict access to provider consoles.
  • Provider security — our service providers apply their own security programmes to the data they hold.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
