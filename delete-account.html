Let8Go — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 19 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the Let8Go mobile game, published by Mina Medhat Kamel (trading as Let8Go), Berlin, Germany.

Let8Go does not have its own account system. If you play competitive tournaments, the account you use is a Skillz account, held by Skillz. That means deletion involves up to two separate requests, described below. Do whichever applies to you — or both.

How to request deletion

1. Your Skillz account (competitive play)

If you have entered Skillz tournaments — free-play or cash — your account, balance, match history and chat messages are held by Skillz, and only Skillz can delete them.

Steps:

  1. Open the Skillz section of Let8Go and go to your Skillz account settings, or contact Skillz support directly.
  2. Request deletion of your Skillz account and associated data.
  3. Follow any identity or eligibility checks Skillz asks for. Because cash competitions are financially regulated, Skillz may need to verify who you are before it can act.

Skillz's process and contact routes are described in the Skillz privacy policy.

We can pass a request on to Skillz on your behalf, but we cannot delete a Skillz account for you, and we cannot see or control what Skillz retains.

2. Data held by Let8Go

Separately, Let8Go collects a small amount of pseudonymous diagnostic and usage data. To have that deleted:

Steps:

  1. Email let8go2026@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us which platform you play on (Android or iOS), and include your app instance identifier or device model if you have it — this helps us locate the right records.
  3. We will confirm receipt and respond within the time limits set by applicable law (under the GDPR, normally within one month).

Because we do not ask you to create a Let8Go account, we may not be able to link any data to you from an email address alone. If we cannot identify any data relating to you, we will tell you.

Data retained after deletion

Some information is kept after a deletion request where retention is legally required. This applies mainly to competitive play:

  • Financial and transaction records — entry fees, prizes and payment records relating to cash competitions. Operators of real-money competitions are required to retain these under financial, tax and anti-money-laundering rules.
  • Fraud prevention and platform integrity — records needed to prevent repeat abuse, cheating or evasion of eligibility rules.
  • Legal obligations and claims — where retention is required by law, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Retention periods for this data are set by Skillz, which acts as its own data controller for competitive play and is subject to its own regulatory obligations. We do not control or set those periods — see the Skillz privacy policy for the applicable terms.

For data held by Let8Go, retention follows the retention settings configured for our Firebase project and Google's own policies. Where we retain anything after a deletion request, we keep only what is necessary and stop using it for any other purpose.

Deleting the app

Uninstalling Let8Go stops any further collection from your device, and removes the push notification token. It does not by itself delete a Skillz account or data already collected — use the request steps above for that.
